## Developer-tool / SaaS benchmark sites
The ones designers point to when arguing about taste. Clean, type-led, dark-mode-done-right, real product visuals (no stock).

- **https://linear.app/** — your pick. The benchmark. Uncompromising minimalism, hero is a real product clip, reads like docs written with taste.
- **https://stripe.com/** — long-form enterprise storytelling; custom illustration system; speaks to devs *and* CFOs.
- **https://vercel.com/** — sharp typography + dark mode done right; spec-first, no fluff for pre-qualified devs.
- **https://www.notion.so/** — playful-with-discipline; the site *is* the product's visual language.
- **https://mercury.com/** — premium minimalism for tech founders; calm, mono, sharply typeset; deliberately *unlike* a bank.
- **https://ramp.com/** — bold saturated color tamed by whitespace + clean type.
- **https://www.anthropic.com/** — quiet academic "paper" aesthetic in a maximalist AI category; cream, careful type, restrained illustrations.
- **https://www.raycast.com/** — dense info, elegant execution; uniform cards, single-color illustration system.
- **https://arc.net/** — brave full-bleed video hero, cinematic "movie trailer" homepage.
- **https://www.retool.com/** · **https://supabase.com/** · **https://resend.com/** · **https://posthog.com/** — frequent teardown subjects for conversion pattern study.

## Award-winning craft / experimental (Awwwards SOTD, Developer Award, CSS Design Awards)
WebGL + GSAP motion-led; the "beauty at 60fps" discipline. Mostly studio/portfolio sites.

- **https://by-kin.com/** — 4 awards (Awwwards SOTD + Developer Award, FWA, CSS WOTD). Restraint, editorial type, weighted smooth scroll.
- **https://iventions.com/** — CSS Design Awards WOTM + Awwwards SOTD/Developer Award. Three.js lighting-as-atmosphere.
- **https://matvoyce.tv/** — kinetic-type artist portfolio; GSAP Site of the Year nominee; type-in-motion.
- **https://uncommonstudio.com.au/** — Awwwards SOTD + Developer Award + FWA; confident grid that breaks at the right moments.
- **https://minhpham.design/** — Awwwards SOTD (dev score 7.77); GSAP over Three.js, 3D that never overwhelms the work.
- **https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_year/** — the live SOTY gallery; **https://www.webdesignawards.io/top-100-websites-2026** — 2026 ranked list (current #1: landearly.com).

## Where to find more (curation hubs)

- **https://land-book.com/** — marketing/landing pages, straight-up.
- **https://www.awwwards.com/** · **https://www.cssdesignawards.com/** — the two award engines.
- **https://designmd.cc/benchmarks** — live design-token extractions (CSS vars, type, spacing) from Stripe/Linear/Vercel/Notion + 60 more — great for stealing real values, not just vibes.

## Quick X/Twitter sentiment (Sep 2026)
- Designers consistently name **Linear, Stripe, Vercel** as the taste benchmarks (designmd/opendesigner "the three most cited when arguing about taste").
- **godly.website** remains the go-to inspo source ("used to be my go-to, wish it wasn't canned").
- 2026 award trend: sites that *feel* interactive, not just look impressive — "beauty at 60fps is the whole discipline."
